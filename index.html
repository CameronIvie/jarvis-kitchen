Things you can say

Tap the brain (or the round button while cooking), then talk.
  • “Short ribs”, “find chicken thighs”, “what can I make with rhubarb”
  • “Open the first one”, “open number two”
  • “Next step”, “go back”, “repeat that”, “read the ingredients”
  • “Make it for 2, tonight and tomorrow’s lunch”, “for 4 people”, “double it”, “half it”
  • “Set a timer for pasta for 9 minutes”, “10 minute timer”, “start the timer” (on a step with a time)
  • “How much time is left?”, “pause the pasta timer”, “add 2 minutes to rice”, “cancel the eggs timer”
  • “Stop” or “dismiss” when an alarm is ringing
  • “Close” or “go home”
On iPhone, voice needs Siri & Dictation turned on, and Safari will ask for the microphone the first time. Speech is turned into text by Apple’s dictation service. On the Pi it will run fully offline.
